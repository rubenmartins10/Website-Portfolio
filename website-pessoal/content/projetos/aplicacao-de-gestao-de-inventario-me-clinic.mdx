**MeClinic** is a modern, comprehensive SaaS (*Software as a Service*) platform built specifically for the professional management of medical clinics and practices. It operates as a true all-in-one "medical ERP", with the primary goal of digitising, centralising and optimising the entire daily operation of a healthcare facility.

**Core Pillars and Features:**

* **Clinical and Operational Management:** The core of the system enables thorough patient registration, maintaining a detailed and secure medical history. Daily operations are orchestrated through an intelligent calendar focused on appointment management and scheduling.
* **Administrative and Financial Control:** The platform automates billing, generating detailed invoices in PDF format. It also integrates an advanced inventory module with barcode scanner support, streamlining the tracking and stock control of clinical supplies.
* **Business Intelligence (Reports):** The system processes clinic data to generate weekly financial and operational analytical reports, dispatched fully automatically by email to management.
* **User Experience (UX):** The interface was built with a focus on modern usability, offering multi-language support and a native *dark mode*, ensuring visual comfort during long work shifts.
* **Compliance and Security (GDPR):** Given the handling of sensitive health data, the platform is *GDPR-compliant*. All access is secured by robust authentication and a strict role-based access control system covering each user type (doctor, receptionist, administrator).
