Incorrupt is a decentralized forensic platform that solves a critical problem in digital evidence management: ensuring that any tampering is immediately detectable. Each submitted piece of evidence generates a cryptographic hash chained to the previous one, forming a hash chain similar to a blockchain but optimized for forensic auditing. Three fully isolated audit nodes independently verify the integrity of each evidence entry, consensus between them is mathematically enforced, eliminating any single point of failure or centralized trust.
